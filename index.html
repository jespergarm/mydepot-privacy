MyDepot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Summary

MyDepot is a local-first application. Your portfolio data, account information and credentials are processed and stored only on your own devices. Nothing is transmitted to the developer or to any third-party analytics, advertising or telemetry service.

What data the app accesses

  • Nordnet account data — positions, transactions, cash balances and account names, retrieved via Nordnet's own interfaces after you sign in with Nordnet Bank's official login flow (QR code via the Nordnet app).
  • Session credentials — a session token issued by Nordnet. It is kept in memory, and optionally stored on disk only in sealed form using Windows DPAPI (bound to your Windows user account).
  • Market metadata — instrument names, sectors, regions and logos fetched from Nordnet's public endpoints and Yahoo Finance to enrich your holdings.

Where the data goes

  • All data stays on your computer or phone under %LOCALAPPDATA%\MyDepot (Windows) or the app's private storage (Android).
  • The app communicates only with nordnet.*, api.prod.nntech.io (Nordnet's backend) and Yahoo Finance endpoints for public market metadata.
  • Your Nordnet session token is sent only to Nordnet's own hosts.
  • There is no developer server. The developer receives nothing — no portfolio data, no credentials, no usage statistics, no crash reports.

Data sharing

None. MyDepot does not sell, share, or transmit personal information to any third party. Nordnet Bank processes your data under its own privacy policy when you use its services.

Data retention and deletion

Because everything is stored locally, you can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app and removing the %LOCALAPPDATA%\MyDepot folder (or the app's storage on Android). Session tokens expire on their own.

Security

  • Local API access is restricted to your machine; LAN access requires a per-session pairing key.
  • Saved sessions are sealed with Windows DPAPI.
  • No advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs are included.

Children

MyDepot is a financial tool intended for adults managing their own brokerage account. It does not knowingly collect data from children.
